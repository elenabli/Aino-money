# Aino Money demo · 3 minutes

Open Aino Money on **Koti** with Sofia selected. Use **Persona → Sofia** if
another profile is active. Refresh before presenting to start with an empty
chat thread.

| Time | Click | What to say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | **Your Aino check-in** | “Aino has already reviewed Sofia’s last 14 days. She can see what changed and compare her current pace with spending 10% or 20% less before payday. Aino connects the difference to her apartment deposit.” |
| 0:30 | **Koti → account → Pay attention** | “Aino flags flexible spending and unexpected essentials for review. Sofia picks a grocery purchase she wants to understand.” |
| 0:55 | **Learn more** on a grocery transaction with a receipt | “Aino opens right on the selected purchase, with its amount, merchant, date and one receipt finding. Sofia can choose what she wants to know next.” |
| 1:20 | **Break down this receipt → See spending in this category** | “The receipt shows what made up this shop. The category total puts the purchase in context, and Sofia can open the transactions behind it.” |
| 2:00 | **More ways to explore → See last month’s dining spending → What if I ate out 20% less?** | “Sofia can test what a different habit might change before committing to it.” |
| 2:40 | **Try 20% less on restaurants for 14 days** | Show the proposed limit and how Sofia can track her progress. |

With an AI provider connected, **Spot a change in my spending** and **Compare
with my usual spending** open a model-backed analysis. The walkthrough above
uses transaction and receipt data directly and works without a provider.
